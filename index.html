Sunday
😴 Rest Night
No actives tonight. Let your skin fully recover from the week. Barrier repair only.
☀️ Morning Routine
1
🧼
Based Daily Facial Cleanser
Based Skincare
📌 How: Wet face, massage in small circles for 30–60 sec, rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry — don't rub.
💡 Why first: Removes overnight oils and dead skin so everything after absorbs properly.
2
💦
Skin Revival Spray
Based Skincare — Hypochlorous Acid
📌 How: Hold 6–8 inches from face, mist evenly, let air dry completely (~30 sec). Do NOT rub in.
💡 Why: Kills bacteria that causes breakouts without stripping your skin. Sets a clean base before moisturizer.
3
👁️
Under Eye Elixir
Based Skincare
📌 How: Use your ring finger (lightest pressure). Gently pat — don't rub or pull — along the under-eye bone.
💡 Why ring finger: The skin here is the thinnest on your face. Tugging causes wrinkles over time.
4
🫧
Daily Facial Moisturizer
Based Skincare — Ceramides + Squalane
📌 How: Pea-sized amount. Warm between fingertips, then press into skin — don't drag across.
💡 Why: Locks in hydration and rebuilds your skin barrier before SPF goes on top.
5
☀️
Anthelios Light Fluid SPF 60
La Roche-Posay — ALWAYS last step
📌 How: Nickel-sized amount. Apply to face and neck. Give 1–2 min to set before going outside.
💡 Why last: SPF needs to sit on top of everything to work. Putting moisturizer over it breaks it down.
⚠️ Non-negotiable every single morning — both BP and Glycolic make your skin burn faster in sun.

🧼
Based Daily Facial Cleanser
Based Skincare
📌 How: Same as morning. Remove the full day — sweat, SPF, pollution. All of it.
💡 Why: Sleeping with SPF/pollution on your face clogs pores and causes breakouts overnight.

💦
Skin Revival Spray
Based Skincare — Hypochlorous Acid
📌 How: Mist on clean skin, air dry. This is your toner replacement — no need for separate toner.
💡 Why: Calms any redness from the day and preps skin to absorb your moisturizer better.
—
No Actives Tonight
Skipping Lira ICE (BP) and LRP Glycolic Serum. Sunday is your skin's full recovery day — no treatments needed.
3
👁️
Under Eye Elixir
Based Skincare
📌 How: Pat gently under eyes. At night this works harder since skin repairs itself while you sleep.
4
🧈
Beef Tallow Moisturizer
Based Skincare — Grass-Fed Tallow + Manuka Honey
📌 How: Warm a small amount between palms, press into face. It's richer than your daily moisturizer — a little goes a long way.
💡 Why tallow on rest night: Its fatty acids mirror your skin's natural oils, deeply repairing your barrier overnight while you sleep.

🌙 Night Routine — BP Night
1
🧼
Based Daily Facial Cleanser
Based Skincare
📌 How: Full cleanse. Remove SPF, sweat, and all buildup from the day before applying any treatments.
💡 Why: BP works on clean skin. Applying it over leftover SPF or grime reduces effectiveness and increases irritation.
2
💦
Skin Revival Spray
Based Skincare — Hypochlorous Acid
📌 How: Mist, air dry. Works with BP to fight acne bacteria from two angles at once.
💡 Why before BP: Prepping skin with HOCL calms any inflammation so BP can do its job without adding extra irritation.
3
🎯
Lira ICE Clarifying Treatment
Lira Clinical — 5% Benzoyl Peroxide
📌 How: One pump. Apply ONLY to active breakout areas — not all over your face. This is a spot treatment, not a full-face serum.
💡 Why spot only: Applying BP all over your face causes unnecessary dryness and peeling in areas that don't need it.
⚠️ Do NOT use with Glycolic Serum — ever. They must always be on separate nights.
—
Skip: LRP Glycolic B5 Serum
Never use Glycolic Acid and Benzoyl Peroxide the same night. The combination causes severe irritation, redness, and over-exfoliation. Glycolic gets its own dedicated nights.
4
👁️
Under Eye Elixir
Based Skincare
📌 How: Pat under eyes only. Apply after BP so the BP is fully settled on its target areas.
5
🧈
Beef Tallow Moisturizer
Based Skincare — Grass-Fed Tallow + Manuka Honey
📌 How: Warm a small amount in palms, press into full face. Wait 5–10 min after applying BP before putting tallow on.
💡 Why tallow specifically on BP nights: BP strips your skin's natural fatty acids. Tallow replenishes them. This is why you use the richer tallow here instead of the lighter daily moisturizer.
⚠️ Use a dark pillowcase tonight — BP bleaches fabric.

🌙 Night Routine — Glycolic Night
1
🧼
Based Daily Facial Cleanser
Based Skincare
📌 How: Full cleanse. Remove all SPF, makeup, and buildup. Glycolic works best on a completely clean face.
💡 Why: Any leftover SPF or oils create a barrier that stops glycolic acid from penetrating evenly, causing patchy results.
2
💦
Skin Revival Spray
Based Skincare — Hypochlorous Acid
📌 How: Mist, air dry fully. Let skin dry completely before applying the glycolic serum — damp skin makes acids absorb faster and can cause irritation.
3
✨
Glycolic B5 Dark Spot Corrector Serum
La Roche-Posay — 10% Glycolic Acid
📌 How: 3–4 drops on fingertips, spread evenly across full face. Avoid eyes and lips entirely. Apply to dry skin only.
💡 Why it works: The 10% glycolic acid dissolves the bonds between dead skin cells and sheds them, revealing fresher skin underneath. The kojic acid and tranexamic acid then target remaining dark spots and discoloration.
⚠️ NEVER use this on the same night as Lira ICE (BP). This is non-negotiable.
—
Skip: Lira ICE Clarifying Treatment (BP)
Glycolic Acid and Benzoyl Peroxide cannot share the same night. Using both causes over-exfoliation, serious irritation, redness, and barrier damage. BP gets Mon/Wed/Fri. Glycolic gets Tue/Thu/Sat.
4
👁️
Under Eye Elixir
Based Skincare
📌 How: Pat under eyes only, after glycolic has settled on skin (~2–3 min). Keep away from eyelids.
5
🫧
Daily Facial Moisturizer
Based Skincare — Ceramides + Squalane
📌 How: Apply over the glycolic serum after it's absorbed (~3–5 min). Lighter than tallow so it doesn't smother the acid's overnight work.
💡 Why lighter moisturizer here: Heavy occlusives like tallow can trap the acid on the skin too intensely. The ceramide moisturizer keeps you hydrated while letting glycolic do its job gently through the night.

⚡ Key Rules
🚫
Never Mix BP + Glycolic the Same Night — Ever
This is the cardinal rule of your entire routine. Benzoyl Peroxide (Lira ICE) and Glycolic Acid (LRP Serum) used together cause severe dryness, redness, peeling, and barrier damage. They work great — just never on the same night. BP is Mon/Wed/Fri. Glycolic is Tue/Thu/Sat. That's it.
🧈
Beef Tallow on BP Nights — Daily Moisturizer on Glycolic Nights
Not interchangeable. BP strips your skin's natural fatty acids — tallow is rich enough to fully replace them overnight. On glycolic nights, the lighter ceramide moisturizer seals hydration without smothering the acid's work. Using tallow on glycolic nights could over-occlude the acid and cause irritation.
☀️
SPF 60 Every Single Morning — No Exceptions
Both Benzoyl Peroxide and Glycolic Acid increase your skin's photosensitivity. On the mornings after glycolic especially, your freshly exfoliated skin will burn faster and dark spots will get WORSE without SPF. The LRP Anthelios goes on last, every morning, regardless of weather.
🛏️
Protect Your Pillowcase on BP Nights (Mon/Wed/Fri)
Benzoyl Peroxide actively bleaches fabric. If you use a white or light pillowcase on BP nights, it will get discolored spots. Use a dark or old pillowcase Mon/Wed/Fri, or wait 10–15 min after applying tallow before lying down.
📈
Build Up Slowly the First Two Weeks
Don't jump straight into the full schedule on Day 1. Week 1: use each active just 1–2x total. Week 2: move to 2–3x. Full alternating schedule from Week 3 onward once your skin has adapted and you know how it's reacting.
💦
Skin Revival Spray Is Your Most Flexible Product
Hypochlorous Acid plays well with literally everything in this routine. Use it AM and PM every day, and use it as a bonus spray after the gym, after touching your face, or anytime your skin feels irritated or inflamed mid-day. No rinsing needed.
